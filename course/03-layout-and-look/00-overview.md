# Layout and look

Where things sit on the page. You will build the top bar of the app and two of its pages, first with classes named for what is on them, and then again with generic classes that you write once and use everywhere. At the end you have a small set of layout classes of your own.

## In this chapter

- [3.1: The top bar](01-the-top-bar.md)\
  Two pages, a bar with links to move between them, and a mark for the page you are on.
- [3.2: The Schedule page](02-the-schedule-page.md)\
  A list of lessons as rows: list tags, `span`, and one child that fills the row.
- [3.3: The Activities page](03-the-activities-page.md)\
  Club cards in a grid that re-flows, and a list of the next sessions.
- [3.4: The same thing twice](04-the-same-thing-twice.md)\
  Move the lines that arrange things into classes named for the arrangement.
- [3.5: One look for many](05-one-look-for-many.md)\
  Write the white box once, as a card, and change one card with one small class.
- [3.6: A scale and a width](06-a-scale-and-a-width.md)\
  A few sizes of space to pick from, a gap you choose for each tag, and a width limit.

Keep the [HTML cheatsheet](../reference/02-html-cheatsheet.md), the [CSS cheatsheet](../reference/03-css-cheatsheet.md) and the [Flexbox and grid cheatsheet](../reference/05-flexbox-and-grid-cheatsheet.md) open while you work.
