# Classes and styles

How to point a style at the right thing, and how every tag is a box with space inside and outside it. At the end your front door is styled, centred, in a card, and reacts to the mouse.

## In this chapter

- [2.1: Give your tags a name](01-give-your-tags-a-name.md)\
  Use classes, so a style reaches some tags and not others.
- [2.2: Style one tag directly](02-style-one-tag-directly.md)\
  The `style` attribute, and when to use it.
- [2.3: Make text look right](03-make-text-look-right.md)\
  Line height, spacing, capitals, bold, and lines through text.
- [2.4: Make the button react](04-make-the-button-react.md)\
  A different look while the mouse is over the button and while it is pressed.
- [2.5: Centre the front door](05-centre-the-front-door.md)\
  Flexbox, and the two axes.
- [2.6: The box](06-the-box.md)\
  Padding, border and margin: the space inside a tag, the line around it, and the space outside.
